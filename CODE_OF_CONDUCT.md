# Code of Conduct

SoraForge is committed to a welcoming, inclusive and professional engineering community.

Be respectful, assume good faith, offer constructive feedback, credit collaborators and keep technical disagreements focused on evidence. Discrimination, harassment, threats, hateful conduct, sexualized harassment and publication of private information are not tolerated.

This policy applies to project issues, PRs, reviews, discussions and other official community spaces. Maintainers may warn participants, hide or remove comments, restrict participation or report serious abuse to GitHub.

For urgent cases, use GitHub's **Report abuse** controls. To report a sensitive project-level misconduct incident, request a private contact method from maintainers **without publishing personal details**. A dedicated confidential reporting contact must be established before expanding the community program.
